Creator content in paid ads, since the account started

Every Meta ad in Kismet's account built from creator or UGC video, April 2024 to 4 November 2026, grouped by creative. Copies of the same video across campaigns and landing pages are added together. Founder and Dr. Kwane whitelisting ads are left out. Purchases and purchase value are what Meta reports on its default attribution. Treat ROAS as a ranking between creatives, not as profit. On overlap with creator code sales: we checked every creator code order in Shopify (103, renewals left out) against where the customer arrived from. 12 came through a paid Meta click ($576), and every one of those clicks was on a Chrissy, John or Dr. Kwane ad, none on a creator ad. So the creator ads here and the code sales on the other tabs barely double count. Meta can also claim sales from people who only saw an ad, which Shopify can't see.

CTR is link clicks over impressions. CPA is spend over Meta-reported purchases. ROAS is purchase value over spend. Hook rate is three-second views over impressions; hold rate is ThruPlays over impressions. Ambassador means the creator is in our Superfiliate programme, matched by handle. Whitelisted means the ad ran under the creator's own Instagram handle, as Superfiliate's Meta Ads view records it. Each link opens the Instagram post the ad was built from. 29 creators have approved Meta partnership access, so Gradient can run their posts as partnership ads.
